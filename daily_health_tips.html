🛡️ Public Health Awareness
💧

Water Intake

Aim for 8 glasses (2-3 litres) of water a day — more if it's hot or you're active.

🏃

Exercise

At least 20-30 minutes of moderate activity most days keeps your heart, weight, and mood in check.

🧼

Hand Washing Guide

Wet hands, apply soap, scrub for 20 seconds (front, back, between fingers, nails), rinse, and dry with a clean cloth.

🥗

Healthy Diet

Fill half your plate with vegetables/fruits, choose whole grains, and limit fried and sugary foods.

🦠

Disease Prevention

Hand hygiene, safe drinking water, mosquito control, and timely check-ups prevent most common illnesses.

💉 Vaccination Awareness

Vaccines train your immune system to recognize and fight specific germs before you're ever exposed — one of the most effective tools in disease prevention.

👶 Childhood Vaccines

BCG, Hepatitis B, OPV/IPV, DPT, Measles-Rubella, and Rotavirus — given as per the national immunization schedule.

🧑 Adolescent & Adult Vaccines

Tetanus-Diphtheria (Td) boosters, HPV (for adolescents), and Hepatitis B for unvaccinated adults.

🧓 Seasonal & High-Risk Vaccines

Annual flu shots, COVID-19 boosters, and pneumococcal vaccines for elderly or immunocompromised individuals.

✈️ Travel Vaccines

Typhoid, Hepatitis A, and Yellow Fever (where required) before travelling to high-risk regions.

⚠️ Vaccine schedules vary by age, region, and medical history — always confirm the right schedule with your doctor or local health centre.
