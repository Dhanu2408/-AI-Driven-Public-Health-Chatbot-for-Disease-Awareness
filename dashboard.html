About this project

AI-Driven Public Health Chatbot is an awareness platform that helps people understand common diseases, their symptoms, and prevention — instantly, in plain language.

Purpose: to make basic health knowledge accessible to everyone, especially where doctor access is limited or delayed.

How it helps: ask the chatbot your symptoms and get possible causes, home care tips, and guidance on when to see a doctor — plus tools like a BMI calculator, disease library, and emergency contacts.

Why it matters: early awareness leads to early action — small daily habits and quick symptom checks can prevent minor issues from becoming serious.
